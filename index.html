About

Z. Khairnar — on location

I’m Zeel — a brand and digital marketing specialist born and raised in Vadodara, working across precision hardware, ad productions and fashion labels — thinking like the marketing desk, and just as happy holding the camera.

My work lives where strategy meets craft. One week I’m writing the positioning document, the next I’m on a shoot set keeping the day on schedule, the next I’m inside an ads manager rebuilding a funnel. Small teams love that range; big teams borrow me for it.

I work from Vadodara with a small circle of collaborators — designers, developers, editors — taking on a handful of launches, rebrands and growth systems a year. If it needs to be sharp and it needs to work, I’m interested.
